# Clarity Wallet
<img width="1376" height="768" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f3b377e7-5231-4fcb-8383-5c4ce45fe28b" />

**Clarity Wallet** is a minimalist, local-first personal finance and net worth tracker for Android. Built with Flutter, it helps you manage your accounts, investments, debts, and daily movements with automatic balance synchronization and complete offline privacy.
---
## Key Features
- **Offline-First & Private:** All data stays locally on your device via SQLite. No cloud tracking, no ads, and no accounts needed.
- **Accounts, Investments & Debts:** Track debit, cash, savings, credit cards (with credit limit usage bars), loans, and investment accounts with one-tap valuation adjustments.
- **Instant Transactions & History:** Log expenses, income, and transfers with atomic balance updates and reversal on edit/delete, plus a searchable history grouped by day with type, account, and category filters.
- **Custom Categories & 4 Minimalist Themes:** Organize your finances with 48 curated icons and 12 accent colors, and switch seamlessly between Light, Warm Paper, Dark Night, and Pure AMOLED themes.
